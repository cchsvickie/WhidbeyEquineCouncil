About Us / Mission

The Whidbey Island Horse Council is a non-profit organization dedicated to preserving equestrian trails, promoting responsible horse ownership, and building a vibrant riding community on Whidbey Island, Washington.

Our mission is to ensure safe, accessible trails for all riders while supporting education, safety, and stewardship of the beautiful lands we share with our horses.

About Us / Mission

The Whidbey Island Horse Council is a group of individuals who volunteer their time to work on issues of importance in protecting, preserving and strengthening access to and use of land on Whidbey Island for equestrian purposes and in perpetuating the equestrian way of life on the island.

Our mission is to advocate for the social, recreational, economic and legislative interests of the Whidbey Island horse community. Keeping horse ownership and enjoyment viable on Whidbey Island is our main goal. The horse community has a positive impact on the island’s economy and environment and we intend to advocate for its continued growth.

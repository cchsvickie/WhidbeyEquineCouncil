Monthly Meetings

The Whidbey Island Horse Council currently operates informally (i.e., is not a registered corporation), is open to anyone who wants to support the Council's mission, and holds monthly meetings during which attendees identify priorities, discuss those of interest to the group, define next steps, allocate responsibilities and report on progress.
